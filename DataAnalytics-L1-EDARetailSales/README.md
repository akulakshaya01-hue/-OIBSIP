# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Internship
Oasis Infobyte – Data Analytics Internship

## Task
Level 1 – Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
Perform exploratory data analysis on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset shape, data types, and missing-value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trends
- Quarterly sales trends
- Customer distribution by age group
- Customer distribution by gender
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation analysis using a heatmap
- Profit analysis by product category
- Business insights and recommendations

## Key Insights
- Customers aged 26–35 form the largest customer segment.
- Rice (5kg) has the highest sales quantity among the top-selling products.
- Electronics generates the highest revenue and profit among product categories.
- Sales amount, profit, and quantity show strong relationships.
- Sales vary considerably across different months and quarters.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus marketing and inventory planning on the Electronics category.
2. Promote high-demand products using targeted offers and bundles.
3. Review pricing and promotional strategies for low-performing categories.
4. Target the 26–35 customer segment with suitable marketing campaigns.
5. Monitor sales fluctuations to improve inventory and promotional planning.
